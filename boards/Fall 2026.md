# Fall 2026 Anime Guide — The Only List That Matters™

*Last updated: October 9, 2026. Sources: Crunchyroll, HIDIVE, Netflix, MyAnimeList, LiveChart, Anime News Network, GameRant, Anime Corner.*

---

## ISEKAI (Truck-kun's Busiest Quarter Yet)

- **Reincarnated as a Sword Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/reincarnated-as-a-sword
    - HIDIVE's flagship fall exclusive (Oct 7). A dad who is legally a sword and his catgirl daughter keep adventuring. The rest of the genre is out here committing tax fraud with cheat skills; this show is just being a wholesome found family. We don't deserve it. Neither does the sword.

- **A Wild Last Boss Appeared! Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00361955/a-wild-last-boss-appeared
    - Gamer wakes up as the final boss of his favorite game — except this server has lore he never read and it's all true (Sep 26, Crunchyroll). History's first player to receive a patch without consent. Nothing humbles a man like becoming somebody else's raid content.

- **A Tale of the Secret Saint**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00371633/a-tale-of-the-secret-saint
    - Girl trains her whole life to become a knight, nearly dies, and conveniently remembers she used to be a world-saving Saint. Twenty years of tutorial grinding, and it turns out she had a max-level account the whole time. Her past life is her cheat code, and she's mortified about it.

- **A Returner's Magic Should Be Special Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GEXH3W207/a-returners-magic-should-be-special
    - Korea's time-loop fantasy returns Oct 7: one guy gets yeeted back before the apocalypse and does the one thing none of us would — homework. He's speedrunning the end of the world via group project. Solo Leveling's studious cousin, still bringing the syllabus.

- **Sasaki and Peeps Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G5PHNM935/sasaki-and-peeps
    - A middle-aged office worker, a psychic bird, and an interdimensional side hustle continue to coexist peacefully. The most realistic isekai on the list: no teenagers, just a tired salaryman diversifying his revenue streams. HR would like a word, Peeps.

- **Magical Explorer**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384003/magical-explorer
    - Guy reincarnates into an adult game as the comic-relief best friend and tries to speedrun the harem with wiki knowledge (Oct 3). White Fox — the studio behind some of anime's most gorgeous tragedies — is animating this. Every studio deserves hazard pay.

- **As a Reincarnated Aristocrat, I'll Use My Appraisal Skill to Rise in the World Season 3** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GG5H5XQMD/as-a-reincarnated-aristocrat-ill-use-my-appraisal-skill-to-rise-in-the-world
    - Third season of the lord whose cheat skill is reading résumés. Other isekai heroes get dragons; this man gets a well-structured org chart and weeps with joy. Talent management remains the genre's most unlikely pillar, and it keeps winning.

- **The World's Strongest Witch**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384009/the-worlds-strongest-witch
    - Kicked out of her party for a "useless" SSS-rank skill called Internet (Oct 7), Lorna now has Google in a world that thinks dew is magic. Knowledge is power; a search engine is a religion. Her old party is presumably still on carrier pigeons.

- **Nia Liston: The Merciless Maiden**
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/nia-liston
    - An undefeated hero reincarnates into a frail noble girl's body and immediately misses hitting people for a living (Oct 6, HIDIVE). The genre's first honest protagonist: she's not here for friendship, she's here for the cooldown. Konami Animation's revenge tour, now with a curtsy.

- **So What's Wrong with Getting Reborn as a Goblin?**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384010/so-whats-wrong-with-getting-reborn-as-a-goblin
    - Office worker dies saving a kid, respawns as a goblin with an 80-year lifespan and a skill that conscripts anyone he beats (Oct 5). In life he climbed the corporate ladder; in death he learned middle management is a martial art. Goblin HR is still HR.

- **Reborn as a Space Mercenary: I Woke Up Piloting the Strongest Starship!**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384006/reborn-as-a-space-mercenary-i-woke-up-piloting-the-strongest-starship
    - Guy wakes up inside his favorite space shooter with a maxed-out ship and a full crew. No revenge arc, no demon lord — he just wants to freelance in peace, making this the season's most responsible power fantasy. A man, his mortgage, and the best spaceship in the galaxy.

- **Even Though I'm a Super Timid Noble Girl, I Accepted the Bet From My Cunning Fiancé**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00365562/even-though-im-a-super-timid-noble-girl-i-accepted-the-bet-from-my-cunning-fianc
    - The season's shyest villainess accepts a bet from her scheming fiancé because apparently "no" hasn't been invented in this kingdom yet (Oct 4). Romance anime's favorite legal system remains the wager. The prenup is a gameshow and feelings are contractually obligated.

- **From Far Away (Kanata kara)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384000/from-far-away
    - A 1994 fantasy classic finally gets animated in 2026 (Crunchyroll, Oct 4): girl falls into another world and accidentally becomes the key to its apocalypse. Vintage isekai — from before the genre had a lawyer. No truck, no cheat menu, just consequences.

## FANTASY (Swords, Sorcery, and HR Violations)

- **Black Clover Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GRE50KV36/black-clover
    - Asta returns Oct 3 after a five-and-a-half-year time skip: still the only person in the kingdom who can't do magic, still the main character, still legally a foghorn. Screaming has never been this structurally important. His lungs are the real magic system.

- **The Apothecary Diaries Season 3** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G3KHEVDJ7/the-apothecary-diaries
    - Maomao is back in the pleasure district doing forensic pharmacy on Oct 2, and Jinshi's family drama has escalated to national stakes. The palace's most dangerous woman remains the one holding the mortar and pestle. Solve the case, refuse the credit, ask about the poison again.

- **Dragon Ball Super: Beerus**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/83189604 *(Netflix — Oct 17; also streaming on Crunchyroll from Oct 11)*
    - Toei "enhances" the original Super with modern visuals — like remastering your uncle's karate footage in 4K. Twenty episodes of a cat god napping between extinction events, now with ray tracing. The franchise doesn't need new ideas, just new pixels.

- **Overgeared**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384004/overgeared
    - Broke gamer inherits a legendary blacksmith class, voluntarily resets his stats to zero, and accidentally becomes the game's entire economy (Oct 2). Watching a man min-craft his way out of debt is the season's most financially relatable power fantasy.

- **The Vermilion Mask (Shuiro no Kamen)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384015/the-vermilion-mask
    - Boy inherits his master's cursed masks, accidentally kills everyone he loves, and keeps collecting them like Infinity Gauntlet pieces (Oct 10). Grief counseling was available. He chose arts and crafts.

- **Romelia War Chronicle**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384007/romelia-war-chronicle
    - Dumped by her prince the literal day after winning the war, Romelia raises an army out of spite and rebuilds a kingdom (Oct 3, two cours). The most ambitious post-breakup glow-up in fiction — other women change their hair; she raised cavalry.

- **Full Clearing Another World Under a Goddess with Zero Believers**
    - (Crunchyroll — Oct 11; series page goes live at premiere)
    - An ex-office worker speedruns a god's game with literally zero followers. He got laid off from one world and pivoted to another — the most LinkedIn-core premise of the season. "Understands the assignment" is an actual divine blessing.

- **Magic Repo Man (Kashita Maryoku wa "Revo Barai" de Kyousei Choushuu)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00385867/magic-repo-man-dumped-by-my-party-ill-cash-in-with-a-cute-support-fairy-to-become-the-strongest
    - A repossessor who takes back magic from deadbeat debtors: the most cathartic premise of the season for anyone who has ever had a loan, a landlord, or a gym membership. Finally, an anime where the collection agency is the protagonist.

- **The Laid-Off Cheat-Granting Mage Enjoys a Second Lease on Life**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384016/the-laid-off-cheat-granting-mage-enjoys-a-second-lease-on-life
    - The guild fires the only guy who can buff everything, so he refunds his buffs, keeps the cheat, and starts his own company (Oct 6). P.A. Works animating the world's pettiest revenge arc. He kept the receipts. HR was not consulted.

- **Welsh & Shedar: The Dofus Chronicles**
    - https://www.youtube.com/@The_Krosmoz *(Free on Ankama's Krosmoz YouTube channel — first two episodes in November, EN/FR/JP audio)*
    - Ankama's Dofus universe gets an anime: a boy cook, a dragon, and a prophecy about the world's tyrant queen. Wakfu's cousin with a bakery — the apocalypse, but served with a sauce. Free on YouTube, because Ankama respects your wallet.

- **Magic Knight Rayearth (2026)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383994/magic-knight-rayearth
    - CLAMP's mecha-mahou-shoujo landmark gets a 30th-anniversary remake (Oct 7). Three schoolgirls, one summoning, and the most devastating plot twist of its decade, now in HD for a brand-new audience to be emotionally ambushed by. Spoiler discipline is a courtesy. Choose carefully.

## ROMANCE (Love Is in the Air — and So Is the Smoke)

- **Blue Box Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81663323
    - Netflix's wholesome badminton slow-burn returns Oct 4, and with the manga finished, every episode now counts double. Taiki remains one confession away from happiness. The planet keeps rotating out of respect for his stamina.

- **Ranma ½ (2024) Season 3** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81171925
    - MAPPA's genderfluid martial-arts romcom hits season 3 on Oct 4. Thirty-plus years later, the fiancée count is still funnier than most current comedies — Takahashi invented the situationship and then weaponized it.

- **Firefly Wedding**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383999/firefly-wedding
    - Dying rich girl proposes to the assassin hired to kill her, and he says yes (Oct 9). David Production turns a hostage situation into the season's most doomed romance. The meet-cute was an ambush; the honeymoon is a crime scene; we're all watching anyway.

- **The Ramparts of Ice Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81991090
    - Netflix's icy school romance continues Oct 1, with walls both literal and emotional — only one of them thaws. Somewhere, a therapist is watching this show and taking billable notes.

- **The Cold Sato-san Is Only Sweet to Me**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384014/the-cold-sato-san-is-only-sweet-to-me
    - The class ice queen with a "salty" reputation melts exclusively for the café boy who stood up for her (Oct 6). The plot: two people pretending photography practice is a date. It's working. Nobody tell them that's just a date.

- **I'm a Witch Who's Crush Requested a Love Potion**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00378072/hello-i-am-a-witch-and-my-crush-wants-me-to-make-a-love-potion
    - Her crush asks the witch to make a love potion — for someone else — so she assigns him side-quests to stall. Weaponized procrastination as a love language. The potion was never the point; the scope creep was.

- **Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle Part 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00364783/chitose-is-in-the-ramune-bottle
    - The sparkly-faced school charmer's second cour drops Oct 13. Being adored by everyone turns out to be a full-time job with no benefits and no union. The show finally admits popularity is shift work.

- **My Girlfriend's Friend (Kanojo no Tomodachi)**
    - https://animefesta.iowl.jp/ *(AnimeFesta exclusive — uncensored Deregura version drops Oct 5)*
    - A twelve-minute-per-episode ecchi drama about — checks notes — the girlfriend's friend. The love triangle is in the title; the consequences are in the runtime; the budget is in your imagination. Listed for completeness, not judgment. (We judge a little.)

- **My Happy Marriage Special Episode**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81585739
    - A three-episode wedding special for Netflix's supernatural newlyweds (Oct 25): just Miyo and Kiyoka being disgustingly happy. No curse, no stakes, just two people being soft. Earned. We'll allow it.

## ACTION (Disagreements Settled at 24 Frames per Second)

- **Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81744227 *(Netflix exclusive — drops Oct 20)*
    - Studio Trigger returns to Night City with a new crew of doomed dreamers. David is gone; the braindance of grief continues. Trigger's brand is making you love someone in eight episodes and charging tears for the ninth. Bring tissues; skip the back-alley cyberware.

- **Tokyo Revengers: War of the Three Titans Arc** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.disneyplus.com/series/tokyo-revengers/4g05R6fVn6sL *(Disney+ exclusive — Oct 3)*
    - Season 4 hits Oct 3: Takemichi time-travels into a three-way gang war somehow more stressful than his actual life. His superpower is crying in the past tense, and he's taken more beatings for character development than most pensions cover.

- **Tougen Anki: Nikko Kegon Falls Arc** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GP5HJ84D2/tougen-anki *(Simulcast via REMOW on Crunchyroll)*
    - Oni-blood delinquent Shiki returns (date TBA) for the waterfall arc. The poster child for "the manga hits different" finally gets anime money and a waterfall budget. Every season needs one demon-blood delinquent show. It's genre law.

- **TANK CHAIR**
    - https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0FYBTVJ1JB731K9U0XDE7U20F1 *(Prime Video exclusive)*
    - Girl assassin on the most dangerous island in the universe wakes her comatose brother by exposing him to murderous intent — and the wheelchair is part of the murder. Polygon Pictures went full abstractionist and nobody stopped them. The title is accurate.

- **HOTEL INHUMANS Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GXJHM3G08/hotel-inhumans
    - The concierge team that patches assassins, forges their papers, and disposes of their evidence is back Oct 4. Five-star service, zero questions asked, and the minibar is free because you just committed a felony. TripAdvisor reviews unavailable.

- **Psyren**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00371866/psyren
    - A payphone, a calling card, and a death game in a ruined future Japan — the 2007 cult manga finally gets its anime (Crunchyroll, October). Gen Z is about to learn what a payphone was. It's 2007 calling, and for once it's good news.

- **Toaru Anbu no ITEM**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383991/a-certain-dark-item
    - The Academy City spinoff nobody expected but the degenerates earned: four dirty-jobs girls doing the missions that never make the news (Oct 9/10). Mugino remains fiction's scariest tsundere and, canonically, its worst boss. Property damage included.

- **Mission: Yozakura Family Season 2 Part 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.disneyplus.com/series/mission-yozakura-family
    - The spy family's second cour continues Oct 11 on Disney+. Their wedding vows legally included "protect each other with extreme prejudice," and family reunions are black-site events. The healthiest marriage in anime is armed.

## DRAMA (For When You Want to Cry On Purpose)

- **Fool Night**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81991076 *(Netflix exclusive — full drop Nov 26)*
    - In a sunless, oxygen-starved future, humans volunteer to become plants. Sunrise×Shaft adapting one of the best manga currently running. Yes, it's a capitalism allegory — "photosynthesize for the company" is the decade's most relatable job offer.

- **BAKI-DOU: The Invincible Samurai**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81922765
    - Netflix brought back resurrected-swordsman Musashi earlier this year; this fall is his victory lap. The man has been dead for four centuries and still can't get a retirement. Physics remains optional; the karate remains unhinged. (Continuing from Part 1 on Netflix)

## SLICE OF LIFE (Cozy Content for Emotionally Unwell Adults)

- **Please Excuse My Younger Brothers (Uchi no Otouto-domo ga Sumimasen)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00371926/please-excuse-my-younger-brothers
    - A girl inherits four stepbrothers, none of whom come with manuals (continuing from Summer). Big-sister energy meets found-family chaos meets the discovery that motherhood was in the fine print. She didn't sign up for this. She's thriving anyway.

- **FX Fighter Kurumi-chan (FX Senshi Kurumi-chan)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384001/fx-fighter-kurumi-chan
    - A college girl day-trades foreign exchange to win back the 20 million yen her late mother lost. A moe anime about margin calls (Oct 1) — the most financially irresponsible and most financially honest show of the season. The candlesticks say a prayer.

- **Ojisan wa Kawaii Mono ga Osuki (Uncle's Obsession with Cute Things)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00385445/uncles-obsession-with-cute-things
    - Handsome boss by day, obsessive cute-mascot collector by night, exposed by one chance encounter (Oct 4). Every adult with a merch shelf felt this premise in their spine. The secret weighs more than the plushies.

- **TETSURYO! Meet with Tetsudou Musume**
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/tetsuryo-meet-with-tetsudou-musume
    - A college girl joins the Railway Travel Club and finds herself via train timetables (Oct 8, HIDIVE). For everyone whose hobby quietly became their entire personality — the trains were the friends we made along the schedule. The sleeper hit, arriving on time.

- **Witch on the Holy Night** *(movie)*
    - (Japan theatrical Nov 19 — NA theaters Jan 29, 2027)
    - TYPE-MOON's visual novel finally hits theaters: a witch, a mage, and two roommates with far too much tragic backstory for one rooftop. Nasu dialogue is 40% poetry, 60% food metaphors, 100% guaranteed to hurt you.

- **The Apothecary Diaries Movie: Bouhi no Hihou (Dec 10, Japan)** *(movie)*
    - Theatrical — streaming TBD
    - A bonus big-screen dose of Maomao (Dec 10, Japan) for anyone whose withdrawal symptoms arrive punctually in December. Poison knowledge included; subtitles not guaranteed. We're all going to pretend we understand Japanese for two hours.

## COMEDY (You Will Laugh. This Is a Threat.)

- **There Is Also a Hole in the Student Organization!**
    - https://oceanveil.net/anime_titles/542 *(OceanVeil exclusive — Aniplex of America, Oct 3)*
    - The title is exactly what it says; no further marketing was deemed necessary. Passione adapts the 4-koma about a student council of weirdos — the genre's wildest workplace simulation. They have budget meetings. And a hole.

- **Choujun! Choujou-senpai (Super Psychic Policeman Chojo)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384013/super-psychic-policeman-chojo
    - A psychic cop uses his powers exclusively to lie, cheat, and dodge parking tickets (Oct 6). Japan's finest. VIZ's gag manga finally gets the dumb buddy-cop anime it deserves — the badge is real, the ESP is wasted, and we wouldn't change a thing.

- **Sgt. Frog (Keroro Gunsou) 2026**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383995/keroro
    - The frog-invasion reboot nobody had on their 2026 bingo card. Twenty-plus years of occupation and Keroro still hasn't conquered Pekopon, mostly because Gundam plastic kits kept happening. The most realistic military sim on TV: defeated by hobby spending.

- **Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom**
    - https://www.primevideo.com/detail/The-Seven-Knights-of-the-Marronnier-Kingdom/0GDXGNY7K1P36U14D0070O8Q1D *(Prime Video exclusive — Oct 3)*
    - Seven knight brothers on a goodwill tour of neighboring kingdoms. J.C. Staff doing a royal boy-band road trip in 25-minute episodes, where diplomacy is mostly being handsome. Somehow it works. The crown's entire budget is the itinerary.

- **DANDIVINE (Jyu-Oh Mujin Dandivine)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00385792/dandivine
    - Twenty years after the combining robot Dandivine saved the world, its toy company's CEO deliberately restarts the war to move merchandise. The most honest satire of the toy industry ever animated: the apocalypse has a SKU number and a pre-order bonus.

- **#I'm Looking for a Zombie**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383996/im-looking-for-a-zombie
    - Post-apocalypse girl goes looking for her dad and discovers the zombie wasteland is honestly kind of a vibe (Oct 3). The sunniest apocalypse this side of Zombieland Saga — the dead walk, the Wi-Fi's down, and she's thriving. Dad better be worth the hike.

- **Ghost Meets Gal!**
    - https://www.youtube.com/@CARDFIGHTVanguard *(Official Cardfight!! Vanguard English short-form stream)*
    - Two-minute episodes of an idol ghost and a gal, streamed by the official Cardfight!! Vanguard channel, of all places. The "dead person befriends living person" genre remains inexhaustible. Content warning: you may get invested in a friendship shorter than a coffee break.

## SCI-FI (Robots, Cyborgs, and Futures We Probably Deserve)

- **DARK MACHINE THE ANIMATION**
    - (Crunchyroll — Oct 13; series page goes live at premiere)
    - An esport secretly controls Japan's economy, a kid chases his grandfather's last words, and the show commits to both with a straight face (Oct 13). Konami's most elaborate product placement since Yu-Gi-Oh convinced children card games were fatal.

- **VERTEX FORCE**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00365619/vertex-force
    - An Okinawan boy with an inferiority complex inherits his sister's "Engine" power just as the world starts ending on schedule (Oct 3). Original mecha, no franchise, no safety net — the rarest Pokémon of all. Nobody gets rich on episode 1 of a robot show; they made it anyway.

- **STEEL BALL RUN JoJo's Bizarre Adventure (2nd STAGE continues)**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/82116553
    - The horse race across America barrels through the Devil's Palm (Netflix, Fridays). Araki's masterpiece gets the big-budget treatment: men pose through national landmarks, the horses do the acting, and the villain's fingernails out-plot most protagonists. Godspeed, David Production's shoulder budget.

- **Detective Conan: 30-Gou Satsujin Jiken (TV special)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GMTE00390308/detective-conan-the-counterfeit-crime-case-of-ultra-30 *(Crunchyroll — streaming as Detective Conan: The Counterfeit Crime Case of Ultra 30; on Netflix Japan)*
    - A two-hour special about counterfeit money for the franchise's 30th anniversary. He's been seven for three decades; the yen note got redesigned before he grew up. The case of the eternal second-grader remains open.

## SPORTS (Sweat, Tears, and Physics Violations)

- **Aoashi Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G4PH0WX5J/aoashi
    - Japan's most tactical soccer anime returns Oct 4 for the World Cup run. Blue Lock for people who understand the most violent thing on the pitch is a well-timed through ball. The xG spreadsheets are canon.

- **The Prince of Tennis II: U-17 World Cup — Final Member Selection Match** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GEXH3W49E/the-prince-of-tennis-ii-u-17-world-cup

- **Ace of Diamond Act II — Second Season Cour 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GYX0MZ58R/ace-of-the-diamond
    - Seido's spring invitational begins Oct 11 and Sawamura finally, FINALLY takes the mound. Baseball boys, emotional damage, and a catcher who has seen everything twice. The pitch count is the character arc.

- **Beyblade X** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81924739
    - Spinning tops remain the most intense sport ever devised about children's toys. The X Tower demands respect, the beystorage demands a mortgage, and a physicist somewhere is weeping. Let it rip, eternally.

## HORROR/THRILLER (Do Not Watch Alone, Do Not Watch Sober, Do Not Tell Your Therapist)

- **Kyoufu Collector (Horror Collector)**
    - (Crunchyroll — Oct 10; series page goes live at premiere)
    - The creepypastas are real, and the boy in the red hood roams town to town collecting the consequences. The rare horror anime that's actually about the internet's ability to ruin a small town. Do not read the comments; that's the whole show.

- **Magical Girl Raising Project: Restart**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GRQ4MKN4Y/magical-girl-raising-project *(Streaming on Crunchyroll)*
    - Sixteen magical girls, ten billion yen, and one elimination mechanic — the death game that's taught distrust of cute outfits since 2016 returns Oct 5. Madoka walked so this could sprint, in combat heels.

- **I'm Dating a Dark Summoner**
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/im-dating-a-dark-summoner
    - HIDIVE's spicy October exclusive: a romcom where the boyfriend summons demons and the girlfriend, somehow, makes it work. The secret to a lasting relationship is not asking what happens in the basement. Love language: eldritch pacts and unbroken eye contact.

- **Kizudarake no Seijo yori Houfuku wo Komete Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.youtube.com/@ItsAnimeJP *(REMOW "It's Anime" official stream — Oct 1)*
    - The short-form saintess revenge fantasy continues. Forgiveness is for people without resurrection magic. Revenge is a dish best served weekly, in short episodes, with a halo.

## OTHER (Where Shows Go When Nobody Cancels Them — Long-Runners & Misc)

- **Detective Conan (Case Closed)** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G6JQVM3ER/detective-conan
    - Still going. The Black Organization has seniority over most real police departments at this point. Conan has solved more murders than some countries have citizens, and the glasses remain load-bearing.

- **One Piece** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GRMG8ZQZR/one-piece
    - Still chasing that pirate treasure. The Egghead-to-Elbaf pipeline keeps flowing; the One Piece remains as theoretical as the ending. Oda will finish right after this arc. And the next one. The journey was the nakama we made along the way.

- **Meitantei Precure! (Star Detective Precure!)** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00369409/star-detective-precure
    - Magical girls solving thefts across a 30-year time slip: Precure's most experimental season yet, and it's working. Somewhere between Conan and Sailor Moon, a new fan is being radicalized into loving mysteries. The transformation sequence is also an interrogation.

- **Red River (Sora wa Akai Kawa no Hotori)** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00378099/red-river
    - The ancient-Hittite-Empire shoujo isekai keeps rolling from Summer into Fall. Yuri remains stuck in the Bronze Age, and the 21st century remains legally and emotionally unavailable. History's most persistent love triangle: one queen, one general, one entire empire.
