# Fall 2026 Anime Guide — The Only List That Matters™

*Last updated: October 9, 2026. Sources: Crunchyroll, HIDIVE, Netflix, MyAnimeList, LiveChart, Anime News Network, GameRant, Anime Corner.*

---

## ISEKAI (Truck-kun's Busiest Season Yet)

- **Reincarnated as a Sword Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/reincarnated-as-a-sword
    - HIDIVE's flagship fall exclusive (Oct 7). A dad who is literally a sword and his catgirl daughter keep adventuring, and somehow it's still the most wholesome found-family show on television.

- **A Wild Last Boss Appeared! Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00361955/a-wild-last-boss-appeared
    - Gamer wakes up as the final boss of his favorite game — except this server has its own lore he never read. The first case of a player getting patched without consent. (Sep 26, Crunchyroll)

- **A Tale of the Secret Saint**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00371633/a-tale-of-the-secret-saint
    - Girl trains her whole life to be a knight, nearly dies, and remembers she used to be a world-saving Saint. Her past life is her OP cheat code and she's *embarrassed* about it.

- **A Returner's Magic Should Be Special Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GEXH3W207/a-returners-magic-should-be-special
    - Korea's time-loop fantasy returns Oct 7: one guy gets yeeted back before the apocalypse and decides to grind the whole party to endgame early. Solo Leveling's studious cousin.

- **Sasaki and Peeps Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G5PHNM935/sasaki-and-peeps
    - A middle-aged office worker, a psychic bird, and an interdimensional side hustle continue. HR would like a word, Peeps.

- **Magical Explorer**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384003/magical-explorer
    - Guy reincarnates into an adult game as the comic-relief best friend and tries to speedrun the harem with wiki knowledge. White Fox animating a self-aware erge romcom — sure, why not. (Oct 3)

- **As a Reincarnated Aristocrat, I'll Use My Appraisal Skill to Rise in the World Season 3** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GG5H5XQMD/as-a-reincarnated-aristocrat-ill-use-my-appraisal-skill-to-rise-in-the-world
    - Third season of the lord whose superpower is reading résumés. HR management as an isekai genre pillar continues.

- **The World's Strongest Witch**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384009/the-worlds-strongest-witch
    - Kicked out for a "useless" SSS-rank skill called Internet, Lorna now has Google in a fantasy world. Knowledge is power; search engines are godhood. (Oct 7)

- **Nia Liston: The Merciless Maiden**
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/nia-liston
    - An undefeated hero reincarnates into a frail noble girl's body and immediately misses hitting people for a living. Konami Animation's own battle-mommy revenge tour. (Oct 6, HIDIVE)

- **So What's Wrong with Getting Reborn as a Goblin?**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384010/so-whats-wrong-with-getting-reborn-as-a-goblin
    - Office worker dies saving a kid, respawns as a goblin with an 80-year lifespan and a skill that conscripts anyone he beats. He climbed the corporate ladder in life; now he's building the ladder. (Oct 5)

- **Reborn as a Space Mercenary: I Woke Up Piloting the Strongest Starship!**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384006/reborn-as-a-space-mercenary-i-woke-up-piloting-the-strongest-starship
    - Guy wakes up inside his favorite space shooter with a maxed-out ship and a full crew. The most responsible power fantasy of the season — he just wants to freelance in peace.

- **Even Though I'm a Super Timid Noble Girl, I Accepted the Bet From My Cunning Fiancé**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00365562/even-though-im-a-super-timid-noble-girl-i-accepted-the-bet-from-my-cunning-fianc
    - The season's shyest villainess accepts a bet from her scheming fiancé because apparently saying "no" wasn't invented yet in this kingdom. (Oct 4)

- **From Far Away (Kanata kara)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384000/from-far-away
    - A 1994 fantasy classic finally gets animated in 2026, streaming on Crunchyroll starting Oct 4: girl falls into another world and accidentally becomes the key to its apocalypse. Vintage isekai, no truck required.

## FANTASY (Swords, Sorcery, and Questionable Life Choices)

- **Black Clover Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GRE50KV36/black-clover
    - Asta returns October 3 after a five-and-a-half-year time skip, still the only person in the kingdom who can't do magic and still the main character. Screaming has never been this powerful.

- **The Apothecary Diaries Season 3** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G3KHEVDJ7/the-apothecary-diaries
    - Maomao is back in the pleasure district doing forensic pharmacy on Oct 2, and Jinshi's family drama now has national stakes. The palace's most dangerous woman remains the one holding a mortar and pestle.

- **Dragon Ball Super: Beerus**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/83189604 *(Netflix — Oct 17; also streaming on Crunchyroll from Oct 11)*
    - Toei "enhances" the original Super with modern visuals, like remastering your uncle's karate footage in 4K. Twenty episodes of cat god naps, now with ray tracing.

- **Overgeared**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384004/overgeared
    - Broke gamer inherits a legendary blacksmith class in VR, resets his stats to zero, and accidentally becomes the game's economy. It's like watching someone min-craft their way out of debt. (Oct 2)

- **The Vermilion Mask (Shuiro no Kamen)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384015/the-vermilion-mask
    - Boy inherits his master's cursed masks, kills everyone he loves by accident, and spends the rest of the show collecting them like Infinity Gauntlet pieces. (Oct 10)

- **Romelia War Chronicle**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384007/romelia-war-chronicle
    - Dumped by her prince the day after the war is won, Romelia raises her own army out of spite and rebuilds a kingdom. Post-breakup glow-up, but with cavalry. (Oct 3, two cours)

- **Full Clearing Another World Under a Goddess with Zero Believers**
    - (Crunchyroll — Oct 11; series page goes live at premiere)
    - An ex-office worker speedruns a god's game with literally no followers. He got laid off from one world and started over in another — the ultimate career pivot.

- **Magic Repo Man (Kashita Maryoku wa "Revo Barai" de Kyousei Choushuu)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00385867/magic-repo-man-dumped-by-my-party-ill-cash-in-with-a-cute-support-fairy-to-become-the-strongest
    - A repossessor who takes back magic from deadbeat debtors. The most cathartic premise of the season if you've ever had a loan in your life.

- **The Laid-Off Cheat-Granting Mage Enjoys a Second Lease on Life**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384016/the-laid-off-cheat-granting-mage-enjoys-a-second-lease-on-life
    - Guild kicks out the only guy who can buff everything; he refunds his buffs, keeps the cheat, and starts his own party. P.A. Works animating the world's pettiest revenge arc. (Oct 6)

- **Welsh & Shedar: The Dofus Chronicles**
    - https://www.youtube.com/@The_Krosmoz *(Free on Ankama's Krosmoz YouTube channel — first two episodes in November, EN/FR/JP audio)*
    - Ankama's Dofus universe gets an anime: a boy cook, a dragon, and a prophecy about the world's tyrant queen. Wakfu's cousin with a bakery.

- **Magic Knight Rayearth (2026)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383994/magic-knight-rayearth
    - CLAMP's 90s mecha-mahou-shoujo landmark gets a 30th-anniversary remake (Oct 7). Three schoolgirls, one summoning, and the most devastating plot twist of the decade, now in HD.

## ROMANCE (Love Is in the Air — and Also on Fire, Probably)

- **Blue Box Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81663323
    - Netflix's wholesome badminton slow-burn returns Oct 4, and the manga literally finished this summer — so every episode now counts double. Taiki still hasn't confessed; the planet keeps rotating out of respect.

- **Ranma ½ (2024) Season 3** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81171925
    - MAPPA's genderfluid martial-arts romcom hits season 3 on Oct 4. Thirty-plus years later, the "fiancée count" is still funnier than most current comedies.

- **Firefly Wedding**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383999/firefly-wedding
    - Dying rich girl proposes to the assassin hired to kill her, and he says yes. David Production turning a hostage situation into the season's most doomed romance (Oct 9).

- **The Ramparts of Ice Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81991090
    - Netflix's icy school romance continues Oct 1. It's got walls, both literal and emotional, and only one of them thaws.

- **The Cold Sato-san Is Only Sweet to Me**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384014/the-cold-sato-san-is-only-sweet-to-me
    - Classmate with a "salty" reputation melts exclusively for the café boy who defends her. She's cold to everyone; the plot is just the two of them pretending photography practice is a date. (Oct 6)

- **I'm a Witch Who's Crush Requested a Love Potion**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00378072/hello-i-am-a-witch-and-my-crush-wants-me-to-make-a-love-potion
    - Witch's crush asks her to make a love potion — for someone else — so she assigns him side-quests to stall. Weaponized procrastination as a love language.

- **Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle Part 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00364783/chitose-is-in-the-ramune-bottle
    - The sparkly-faced school charmer's second cour drops Oct 13, where being popular is a full-time job with no benefits.

- **My Girlfriend's Friend (Kanojo no Tomodachi)**
    - https://animefesta.iowl.jp/ *(AnimeFesta exclusive — uncensored dereglua version drops Oct 5)*
    - A 12-minute-per-episode ecchi drama about, per the title, the girlfriend's friend. The love triangle is in the title; the consequences are in the runtime.

- **My Happy Marriage Special Episode**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81585739
    - A three-episode wedding special for Netflix's supernatural newlyweds (Oct 25). It's just Miyo and Kiyoka being disgustingly happy for three episodes, and honestly? Earned.

## ACTION (Punching, Slashing, and General Mayhem)

- **Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81744227 *(Netflix exclusive — drops Oct 20)*
    - Studio Trigger's return to Night City, Oct 20, with a new crew of doomed dreamers. David is gone; the braindance of grief continues. Bring tissues and a refusal to get cyberware from a back alley.

- **Tokyo Revengers: War of the Three Titans Arc** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.disneyplus.com/series/tokyo-revengers/4g05R6fVn6sL *(Disney+ exclusive — Oct 3)*
    - Season 4 hits Oct 3: Takemichi time-travels into a three-way gang war that's somehow more stressful than his actual life. Man keeps getting beaten up for character development.

- **Tougen Anki: Nikko Kegon Falls Arc** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GP5HJ84D2/tougen-anki *(Simulcast via REMOW on Crunchyroll)*
    - Oni-blood delinquent Shiki returns Oct (TBA) for the waterfall arc. The poster child for "the manga was better" finally getting anime money.

- **TANK CHAIR**
    - https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0FYBTVJ1JB731K9U0XDE7U20F1 *(Prime Video exclusive)*
    - Girl assassin on the most dangerous island in the universe wakes her comatose brother by exposing him to murderous intent — and the wheelchair is part of the murder. Polygon Pictures going full abstractionist.

- **HOTEL INHUMANS Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GXJHM3G08/hotel-inhumans
    - The concierge team that treats assassins' wounds, forges their IDs, and disposes of their evidence is back Oct 4. Five-star service, zero questions asked.

- **Psyren**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00371866/psyren
    - A payphone, a calling card, and a death game in a ruined future Japan — the 2007 cult manga finally gets its anime (Crunchyroll, October). Answer the phone; it's 2007 calling with good news.

- **Toaru Anbu no ITEM**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383991/a-certain-dark-item
    - The Academy City spinoff nobody expected but degenerates deserve: four dirty-jobs girls (Oct 9/10) doing the missions that never make the news. Mugino remains the scariest tsundere in fiction.

- **Mission: Yozakura Family Season 2 Part 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.disneyplus.com/series/mission-yozakura-family
    - The spy family's second cour continues Oct 11 on Disney+. Wedding vows included "protect each other with extreme prejudice."

## DRAMA (Feelings, Feuds, and Existential Crises)

- **Fool Night**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81991076 *(Netflix exclusive — full drop Nov 26)*
    - Humans volunteer to become plants in a sunless, oxygen-starved future (Nov 26, full drop). Sunrise×Shaft adapting one of the best manga running. Yes, the body horror is also a capitalism allegory.

- **BAKI-DOU: The Invincible Samurai**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81922765
    - Netflix brought back resurrected-swordsman Musashi earlier in 2026 — this fall is his victory lap. (Continuing from Part 1 on Netflix)

## SLICE OF LIFE (The Mundane, But Make It Anime)

- **Please Excuse My Younger Brothers (Uchi no Otouto-domo ga Sumimasen)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00371926/please-excuse-my-younger-brothers
    - Girl inherits four stepbrothers, none of whom come with manuals. Big sister energy meets found-family chaos. (Continuing from Summer)

- **FX Fighter Kurumi-chan (FX Senshi Kurumi-chan)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384001/fx-fighter-kurumi-chan
    - College girl day-trades foreign exchange to recover the 20 million yen her late mother lost. It's a moe anime about margin calls (Oct 1). The most financially irresponsible watch of the season.

- **Ojisan wa Kawaii Mono ga Osuki (Uncle's Obsession with Cute Things)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00385445/uncles-obsession-with-cute-things
    - Handsome boss by day, obsessive cute-mascot collector by night, outed by one chance encounter. The relatable taxman of hobbies. (Oct 4)

- **TETSURYO! Meet with Tetsudou Musume**
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/tetsuryo-meet-with-tetsudou-musume
    - College girl joins the Railway Travel Club and finds herself via train timetables (Oct 8, HIDIVE exclusive). The sleeper hit for anyone whose hobby got a life of its own.

- **Witch on the Holy Night** *(movie)*
    - (Japan theatrical Nov 19 — NA theaters Jan 29, 2027)
    - TYPE-MOON's visual novel finally hits theaters: a witch, a mage, and far too much tragic backstory for one rooftop.

- **The Apothecary Diaries Movie: Bouhi no Hihou (Dec 10, Japan)** *(movie)*
    - Theatrical — streaming TBD
    - A bonus big-screen dose of Maomao for anyone whose withdrawal symptoms start in December.

## COMEDY (You Will Laugh, This Is a Threat)

- **There Is Also a Hole in the Student Organization!**
    - https://oceanveil.net/anime_titles/542 *(OceanVeil exclusive — Aniplex of America, Oct 3)*
    - Title is exactly what it says. Passione adapting the 4-koma about a student council full of weirdos — the "student council" genre continues to be the wildest workplace simulation in anime.

- **Choujun! Choujou-senpai (Super Psychic Policeman Chojo)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00384013/super-psychic-policeman-chojo
    - A psychic cop uses his powers exclusively to lie, cheat, and get out of parking tickets (Oct 6). VIZ's gag manga finally gets the dumb buddy-cop anime it deserves.

- **Sgt. Frog (Keroro Gunsou) 2026**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383995/keroro
    - The frog invasion reboot nobody predicted. Twenty-plus years later and Keroro still hasn't conquered Pekopon, which is honestly the most realistic military simulation on TV.

- **Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom**
    - https://www.primevideo.com/detail/The-Seven-Knights-of-the-Marronnier-Kingdom/0GDXGNY7K1P36U14D0070O8Q1D *(Prime Video exclusive — Oct 3)*
    - Seven knight brothers on goodwill tours across neighboring kingdoms. J.C. Staff doing a royal-boys' road trip in 25-minute episodes, and somehow it works.

- **DANDIVINE (Jyu-Oh Mujin Dandivine)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00385792/dandivine
    - Twenty years after the combining robot Dandivine saved the world, its toy company's CEO *deliberately* restarts the war to boost sales. The most honest satire of the mecha toy industry ever animated.

- **#I'm Looking for a Zombie**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00383996/im-looking-for-a-zombie
    - Post-apocalypse girl goes looking for her dad and finds out the zombie wasteland is actually pretty fun. The sunniest apocalypse this side of Zombieland Saga. (Oct 3)

- **Ghost Meets Gal!**
    - https://www.youtube.com/@CARDFIGHTVanguard *(Official Cardfight!! Vanguard English short-form stream)*
    - Two-minute episodes of an idol ghost and a gal. The genre of "a dead person and a living person get along" is truly inexhaustible.

## SCI-FI (Robots, Cyborgs, and Questionable Futures)

- **DARK MACHINE THE ANIMATION**
    - (Crunchyroll — Oct 13; series page goes live at premiere)
    - An esport controls Japan's economy, a kid chases his grandfather's last words, and the stakes are somehow both deeply silly and fully committed (Oct 13). Konami's most elaborate product placement.

- **VERTEX FORCE**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00365619/vertex-force
    - Okinawan boy with an inferiority complex inherits his sister's "Engine" power when global mysteries start (Oct 3). Original mecha, no franchise, no warning — the rarest Pokémon of all.

- **STEEL BALL RUN JoJo's Bizarre Adventure (2nd STAGE continues)**
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/82116553
    - The Netflix-exclusive horse race across America barrels through the Devil's Palm starting Sept 25 (Fridays). Araki's masterpiece gets the big-budget treatment; the horses remain the real protagonists.

- **Detective Conan: 30-Gou Satsujin Jiken (TV special)**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GMTE00390308/detective-conan-the-counterfeit-crime-case-of-ultra-30 *(Crunchyroll — streaming as Detective Conan: The Counterfeit Crime Case of Ultra 30; on Netflix Japan)*
    - A two-hour Conan special about counterfeit money for the franchise's 30th anniversary. He's been seven for three decades; the yen note got redesigned before he grew up.

## SPORTS (Sweat, Tears, and Suspenseful Lighting)

- **Aoashi Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G4PH0WX5J/aoashi
    - Japan's most tactical soccer anime returns Oct 4 for the World Cup run. Blue Lock for people who actually like midfield structure.

- **The Prince of Tennis II: U-17 World Cup — Final Member Selection Match** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GEXH3W49E/the-prince-of-tennis-ii-u-17-world-cup
    - Before the World Cup final, teammates literally have to play each other for roster spots (Sep 30). The most cutthroat tryout in sports anime history, and yes, someone will serve a tennis ball that splits a meteor.

- **Ace of Diamond Act II — Second Season Cour 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GYX0MZ58R/ace-of-the-diamond
    - Seido's spring invitational begins Oct 11 and Sawamura finally takes the mound. Baseball boys, emotional damage, and a catcher who's seen everything twice.

- **Beyblade X** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.netflix.com/title/81924739
    - Spinning tops remain the most intense sport about children's toys. The X Tower demands respect.

## HORROR/THRILLER (Do Not Watch Alone, Do Not Watch Sober)

- **Kyoufu Collector (Horror Collector)**
    - (Crunchyroll — Oct 10; series page goes live at premiere)
    - Creepypastas are real, and the boy in the red hood roams town to town collecting the consequences. The rare horror anime that's actually about the internet's ability to ruin small towns.

- **Magical Girl Raising Project: Restart**
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GRQ4MKN4Y/magical-girl-raising-project *(Streaming on Crunchyroll)*
    - Sixteen magical girls, ten billion yen, and an elimination mechanic — the death game that made everyone distrust cute outfits since 2016 returns Oct 5. Madoka walked so this could sprint.

- **I'm Dating a Dark Summoner**
    - https://www.hidive.com/tv/im-dating-a-dark-summoner
    - HIDIVE's spicy October exclusive: romcom where the boyfriend summons demons and the girlfriend, somehow, makes it work. Love language: eldritch pacts.

- **Kizudarake no Seijo yori Houfuku wo Komete Season 2** *(sequel)*
    - https://www.youtube.com/@ItsAnimeJP *(REMOW "It's Anime" official stream — Oct 1)*
    - The short-form saintess revenge fantasy continues. Forgiveness is for people without resurrection magic.

## OTHER (The "Where Do We Put This?" Section — Long-Runners & Misc)

- **Detective Conan (Case Closed)** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G6JQVM3ER/detective-conan
    - Still going. At this point the Black Organization has seniority over most real police departments.

- **One Piece** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GRMG8ZQZR/one-piece

- **Meitantei Precure! (Star Detective Precure!)** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00369409/star-detective-precure

- **Red River (Sora wa Akai Kawa no Hotori)** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GT00378099/red-river
    - The ancient-Hittite-Empire shoujo isekai keeps airing from Summer into Fall. Yuri still hasn't been released to the 21st century, legally or emotionally.

- **Rilakkuma** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GEXH3W778/rilakkuma

- **Odekake Kozame Season 2** *(continuing)*
    - https://www.youtube.com/@odekake-kozame

- **Pocket Monsters (2023) / Pokémon Horizons** *(continuing)*

- **BanG Dream! Ave Mujica: Prima Aurora (Oct 15)** *(movie)*
